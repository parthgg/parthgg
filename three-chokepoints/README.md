## Facts and sources
- Hormuz: ~20 M bbl/day in 2024, ~20% of global petroleum liquids consumption (U.S. EIA). Narrowest ~39 km (measured from elevation data).
- Bab el-Mandeb: 8.7 M bbl/day (2023) to 4.0 M bbl/day (Jan-Aug 2024) after attacks from Nov 2023; Cape route ~15 days longer (U.S. EIA, Vortexa data). Narrowest ~26 km.
- Malacca: Phillips Channel ~2.7 km (EIA); 102,500+ ships in 2025 (Marine Department Malaysia, via news reports); 23.2 vs Hormuz 20.9 M bbl/day, H1 2025 (EIA, via news reports); ~80% of China's oil imports (widely cited estimate).
