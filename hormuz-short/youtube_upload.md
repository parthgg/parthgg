## Description
Every tanker leaving the Persian Gulf has to pass through the Strait of Hormuz, a channel only about 39 km (21 nautical miles) wide at its narrowest point. Ships use two lanes, one inbound and one outbound, each about 2 miles wide. Roughly 20 million barrels of oil flow through every day, about a fifth of global oil consumption.
